## 📝 Notes & Reminders

### General Tips:
- **Start early**: Many applicants underestimate the time required
- **Customize everything**: Generic applications rarely succeed
- **Get feedback**: Have professors review your materials
- **Follow up**: Check application status regularly
- **Stay organized**: Use a spreadsheet to track everything
- **Be honest**: Don't exaggerate, but don't undersell yourself
- **Proofread**: Multiple times, by multiple people

### Red Flags to Avoid:
- ❌ Missing deadlines
- ❌ Generic, copy-pasted SOPs
- ❌ Incomplete applications
- ❌ Poor English in materials
- ❌ Not following instructions
- ❌ Forgetting to submit recommendation letters
- ❌ Applying to programs without research

### Green Flags to Emphasize:
- ✅ Specific knowledge of program
- ✅ Clear career goals
- ✅ Demonstrated passion through projects
- ✅ Realistic self-assessment
- ✅ Cultural fit with program
- ✅ Strong quantitative background
- ✅ Entrepreneurial achievements
