## 1. Erasmus Mundus Scholarships (Fully Funded) 🌟

### What is Erasmus Mundus?
Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters are prestigious, fully-funded programs where you study at multiple European universities over 2 years. They're **highly competitive** but offer the best funding packages for data science students.

### Available Programs for Data Science (2027 intake):

#### a) DEAI - Data Engineering & Artificial Intelligence
**Universities**: 
- Université Libre de Bruxelles (Belgium) - Semester 1
- Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya, Barcelona (Spain) - Semester 2
- Choice for Semester 3: TU Wien (Austria), Lyon 1 (France), or Padua (Italy)
- Semester 4: Master's thesis at chosen university

**Duration**: 2 years (120 ECTS)

**Funding Coverage**:
- Monthly allowance: €1,400 for 24 months (€33,600 total)
- Participation costs: €9,000 per year
- Health insurance
- Travel and visa costs covered
- **Total value**: ~€51,600

**Application Deadline**: 
- **Scholarship deadline**: 11 January 2027 at 11:00 UTC
- **Reference letters due**: 18 January 2027
- Self-funded (2nd round): 15 May 2027 (non-EU), 30 May 2027 (EU)

**Eligibility**:
- 180 ECTS bachelor's degree (preferably Computer Science)
- English B2 level (e.g., IELTS Academic 5.5, TOEFL PBT 570)
- Open to ALL nationalities
- Cannot have received previous Erasmus Mundus JMDEGREE scholarship

**Self-funded costs** (if not awarded scholarship):
- EU students: €4,500/year
- Non-EU students: €9,000/year

**Selection Criteria**:
- Bachelor's subject relevance (CS preferred)
- Academic grades
- Other studies and certifications
- Work experience
- Cover letter quality
- Reference letters

**Application Requirements**:
- Online application form
- Degrees with ECTS and marks
- Work experience documentation
- English proficiency proof
- 1-3 referee contact information
- Cover letter

## 2. National University Scholarships

### KTH Scholarship (Sweden)
**Program**: MSc Machine Learning

**Coverage**: Full tuition fee (1 or 2 years)
- Does NOT cover living costs
- ~€10,000-12,000/year needed for living expenses in Stockholm

**Selection Rate**: 9% of applicants (65 out of 718 in 2026)

**Focus**: Academic excellence + contribution to sustainable development

**Application Deadline**: 15 January 2027

**Scholarship Results**: 1 April 2027

**Eligibility**:
- Fee-paying student
- Applied for KTH master's programme as FIRST priority on universityadmissions.se
- Paid application fee
- Available for 2-year programs and some joint programs
- NOT available for Erasmus+, EIT programs, or Computer Simulations program

**Application Process**:
1. First apply to KTH master's program via universityadmissions.se
2. Submit scholarship application via KTH form (when open)
3. Documents from universityadmissions.se will be used for evaluation
4. Documents must be submitted by 1 February

**Alternative**: KTH Joint Programme Scholarship for some joint programs

## 3. DAAD Scholarships (Germany) - For Czech Citizens

### Study Scholarships - Master Studies for All Academic Disciplines

**Eligibility**: Czech citizens who have completed (or will complete) their first degree (Bachelor's) by funding start

**What Can Be Funded**:
- Complete postgraduate/Master's degree in Germany at state or state-recognised university, OR
- One year of study in Germany as part of Master's degree at home university (credits must be recognised)

**Duration**: 10-24 months depending on program length
- For 2-year programs: Apply for full 2 years
- After first year: Performance assessed; if successful, scholarship continues

**Funding Must Begin**: 2027 (usually 1 October 2027, or earlier if taking German course)

**Monthly Stipend**: €992
- Plus additional allowances
- Full tuition waiver (German public universities are tuition-free)

**Application Deadline**: **16 November 2026** (ALREADY PASSED for 2027/28)
- **Note**: You may have missed this deadline. Check if late applications are possible or plan for 2028/29 intake.

**Application Documents** (for reference for next cycle):
- Letter of motivation (1-3 pages)
- Academic and personal reasons for studying in Germany
- Statement about study project
- Bachelor's degree and transcripts
- German language certificate (if applicable)
- Letter of admission from German university (can be submitted later if not yet received)

**Alternative DAAD Option**: Study Scholarships for STEM Disciplines
- Similar benefits
- Specifically for mathematics, computer science, natural sciences, engineering
- For developing/emerging countries primarily (Czech Republic may not qualify)
- Also requires completion by application deadline

---

### DAAD Important Note:
The deadline for 2027/28 has already passed (16 Nov 2026). However, you can:
1. Check DAAD Czech Republic website for any late application possibilities
2. Plan ahead for 2028/29 intake (application would be Nov 2027)

## Scholarship Success Tips

### For Erasmus Mundus Programs:
1. **Start early** - Applications take time (transcripts, letters, essays)
2. **Strong academic record** - Usually need 3.5+ GPA or top 20% of class
3. **Relevant background** - CS/Engineering/Math backgrounds score highest
4. **Compelling motivation** - Why this consortium? Why multiple countries? Career goals?
5. **Strong references** - Academic references from professors who know your work well
6. **Work/research experience** - Your Bluebee startup and projects are excellent
7. **Quantitative skills** - Highlight your ML, neural networks, analytics experience

### For National Scholarships (KTH, TU/e):
1. **Sustainable development angle** - KTH values this; connect your work to societal impact
2. **Academic excellence** - Need high GPA (7.5+/10 for TU/e)
3. **Clear career goals** - Show how the program fits your trajectory
4. **Research interests** - Demonstrate interest in cutting-edge topics

### For DAAD (Future Applications):
1. **Strong academic ties** - If you've collaborated with German institutions
2. **Cultural exchange** - Show interest in German academic culture
3. **Return contribution** - How you'll contribute to Czech Republic after studies
4. **Language** - German language skills are a plus (but not required for English programs)
