## Detailed Program Information

### 🇭🇺 Hungary

#### ELTE Budapest - MSc Data Science
- **Cost**: €3,200/semester
- **Application Fee**: €150
- **Language**: English (B2 required)
- **Requirements**: 
  - BSc in Computer Science OR
  - 60 ECTS in informatics/mathematics subjects
  - Entrance exam required
- **Application Materials**:
  - Secondary school certificate
  - Bachelor degree & transcript
  - CV, motivation letter
  - Passport copy & photo
  - Language certificate (preferred but not required)
  - Proof of application fee

### 🇱🇹 Lithuania

#### Vilnius University - MSc Data Science
- **Cost**: €6,000/year
- **Application Fee**: €100
- **Language**: English (IELTS 6.5+, TOEFL iBT 4.5+)
- **Requirements**:
  - Bachelor degree (gap since completion ≤ 5 years for non-EU/EFTA)
  - Knowledge of: Calculus, Matrix Algebra, Probability, Statistics, Informatics (R/Python)
  - Entrance exam: dataset analysis + interview
  - 2+ references
  - Motivation letter
  - Non-EU (except OECD, Singapore, South Africa, Ukraine) must pass OMPT-E with 70%+

### 🇩🇪 Germany

#### SRH Heidelberg - MSc Applied Data Science & AI
- **Cost**: €6,750/semester + €1,000 registration
- **Language**: English (B2: IELTS 6.5, TOEFL iBT 87, Duolingo 110)
- **Requirements**:
  - 180 ECTS bachelor's in CS, data science, IT, AI, data analysis, math, or business informatics
  - Python/R proficiency

#### Frankfurt School - MSc AI & Data Science
- **Cost**: €35,500 total + €600 enrolment + €100 application
- **Language**: English (TOEFL 5.0 iBT, IELTS 7.0)
- **Requirements**:
  - 180+ ECTS bachelor's
  - Strong quantitative skills
  - GMAT/GRE or Frankfurt School Admission Test
  - Admissions interview

#### TU Munich - MSc Data Engineering & Analytics
- **Cost**: Free (public university)
- **Language**: English
- **Application Materials**:
  - Bachelor certificate & transcript
  - Statement of reasons (max 2 pages)
  - CV
  - Scientific essay (~1,000 words)
  - Curriculum analysis form
  - Curriculum/module descriptions
  - English proficiency proof
  - VPD from uni-assist (if degree outside Germany)
  - GRE/GATE (if from Bangladesh, China, India, Iran, Pakistan)

## Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters (Fully Funded)

### DEAI - Data Engineering & AI
- **Universities**: ULB (Belgium), UPC (Spain), TU Wien (Austria), Lyon 1 (France), Padua (Italy)
- **Duration**: 2 years (120 ECTS)
- **Cost**: €4,500/year (EU self-funded); €9,000/year (non-EU self-funded)
- **Scholarship**: €1,400/month + tuition + insurance = €51,600 total
- **Deadline**: 11 Jan 2027 (scholarship); references due 18 Jan 2027
- **Requirements**:
  - 180 ECTS bachelor's (preferably CS)
  - English B2 (e.g., IELTS Academic 5.5)

### CoDaS - Communications Engineering & Data Science
- **Duration**: 2 years
- **Countries**: Finland, France, Portugal, Germany, Spain
- **Scholarship**: €1,400/month + tuition + insurance
- **Deadline**: 5 Jan 2027, 15:00 GMT+2
- **Requirements**: English B2 (IELTS Academic)

### EDISS - Engineering of Data-Intensive Intelligent Software Systems
- **Duration**: 2 years
- **Scholarship**: Up to €51,600 (€1,400/month + €9,000/year participation cost)
- **Deadline**: 12 Jan 2027 (scholarship)
- **Application Opens**: 16 Nov 2026
