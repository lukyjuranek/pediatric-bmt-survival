## Writing Tips

### Do:
- ✅ Use specific examples and metrics
- ✅ Show, don't tell ("I deployed a model that processed X requests" vs "I'm good at ML")
- ✅ Connect every technical skill to an outcome
- ✅ Demonstrate self-awareness about what you need to learn
- ✅ Be authentic—your entrepreneurial story is genuinely unique
- ✅ Proofread multiple times
- ✅ Have 2-3 people review it

### Don't:
- ❌ Use generic statements ("I'm passionate about data science")
- ❌ List technologies without context
- ❌ Be overly humble (this is your chance to showcase achievements)
- ❌ Be arrogant (balance confidence with humility)
- ❌ Copy-paste the same SOP for all programs
- ❌ Exceed word limits
- ❌ Use clichés ("since childhood I've been fascinated...")
